What “flight ready” means

From the hatcher
to a hard flush.

Bobwhite eggs take about 23 days to hatch. Chicks are moved into flight pens at around six weeks, away from people and dogs, so they grow into strong, wary flyers. Most buyers want them at 12 to 16 weeks.

General guidance from MSU Extension, Extension.org and Gun Dog. Ask the farm about its own schedule.
