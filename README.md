# B & T Quail Farm

Website for **B & T Quail Farm**, Putnam County, Indiana: flight ready quail for dog training and population purposes.

Live: https://justuseflint.github.io/bt-quail-farm/

## Pages
- `/`: home: the farm, what "flight ready" means, the 4,300-egg hatch, the two uses, photos, and Ethan & Trevor
- `/birds/`: dog training and population/release, with Indiana rule checklists and field notes
- `/about/`: Ethan & Trevor, the farm in numbers, and what the seal stands for
- `/contact/`: a bird inquiry builder that prepares a text to Ethan or Trevor, plus an FAQ
- `/404.html`

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JS generated by `node _build/build.mjs`. GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, MotionPath, DrawSVG) and Lenis are vendored in `assets/vendor/`. Page transitions use cross-document View Transitions where supported, with a lightweight CSS/JS fallback.
