## Bobwhite biology / raising (general, not farm-specific)
- Incubation 23 days for bobwhite (range 23–24). MSU Extension: https://extension.msstate.edu/agriculture/livestock/poultry/incubation-duration-periods ; hatchery guide table: https://extension.msstate.edu/agriculture/livestock/poultry/hatchery-management-guide-for-game-bird-and-small-poultry-flock-owners
- MSU "Raising Bobwhite Quail for Commercial Use": move eggs to hatcher on day 21; no turning after day 19; flight-pen birds one month before release; birds considered mature at 16 weeks; min 2 sq ft per bird in flight pen; "isolation, not pen length, is the key to having a flighty bird"; release area needs cover, food, water. https://www.poultry.msstate.edu/pdf/extension/raising_quail.pdf
- Extension (poultry.extension.org): move to flight pens at ~6 weeks; preserves buy at 15–16 weeks; at least 6 weeks in flight pens before release. https://poultry.extension.org/articles/poultry-management/raising-bobwhite-quail-small-and-backyard-flock/
- Gun Dog mag: "A flight-ready adult is around 12 to 16 weeks... Most people want them at 16 weeks." https://www.gundogmag.com/editorial/pros-cons-raising-quail/543529
- NBGI life history: incubation 23 days; chicks walk within an hour; first fly ~2 weeks; adult size 3–4 months. https://nbgi.org/bobwhites/life-history/
- Pen-raised release survival is generally low; habitat is the main driver of wild populations (NBGI; Texas A&M NRI). https://nbgi.org/2013/10/31/shells-covert-on-science-pen-raised-quail-hope/ , https://nri.tamu.edu/blog/2018/july/why-dont-pen-raised-quail-survive-in-the-wild

## Indiana rules (cite; the farm's own license status is NOT known)
- Indiana DNR Game Breeder's License info: needed to sell bobwhite or possess >5 days; $25/yr raise/possess/release, $30/yr to sell; apply within 5 days of possession; enclosure inspection by conservation officer; keep inventory/transaction log; receipts for birds sold/transferred; bobwhite may be released on property where you have permission if not sick/diseased. https://www.in.gov/dnr/fish-and-wildlife/files/fw-game-breeder-license-information.pdf ; laws: https://www.in.gov/dnr/fish-and-wildlife/files/fw-Game_Breeders_License_Laws.pdf ; 312 IAC 9-10-4 https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/indiana/312-IAC-9-10-4
- Dog Training Ground Permit (312 IAC 9-10-16): needed to take captive-raised bobwhite/pheasant for dog training; 5–20 contiguous acres; not within 1 mile of state public hunting area; boundary signs 16"x16" white, "dog training ground" 1" letters, ≤500 ft apart; band birds (metal/plastic leg band) before release; carry bill of sale or copy of game breeder license; daily record (trainer name/address, number & species released, taken); take birds only sunrise–sunset; can't hold birds >5 days without game breeder license. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/indiana/312-IAC-9-10-16 ; https://www.in.gov/dnr/fish-and-wildlife/files/fw-DogTrainingGroundPermitReg.pdf
